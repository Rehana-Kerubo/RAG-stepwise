# Common Onboarding Troubleshooting

**Device won't activate:** Check SIM data balance or Wi-Fi connection. Restart the device. Re-enter the activation code. If it still fails after 2 attempts, escalate to technical support with the device serial number.

**Transaction fails repeatedly:** Confirm the Till/Paybill number is correctly linked and KYC is approved. Ask the merchant if their linked bank account or wallet is active. Network congestion can also cause temporary failures; retry after a few minutes.

**Merchant not receiving SMS confirmations:** Verify the phone number on file is correct and has no typos. Ask the merchant to check if their phone is blocking unknown SMS senders.

**KYC rejected:** Re-check the reason stated in the rejection SMS. Most rejections are due to blurry photos or a name mismatch between the ID and the business registration. Re-collect documents carefully and resubmit.

**Staff PIN not working:** Confirm the staff account was created correctly and the SMS with the PIN was received. PINs expire after 24 hours if unused; generate a new one from Staff Management if needed.

If an issue is not resolved after following these steps, escalate to the regional support line rather than attempting further fixes on-site.
