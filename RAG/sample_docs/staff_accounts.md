# Setting Up Staff Accounts

Merchants with employees who will operate the POS device need individual staff accounts, rather than sharing the owner's login.

To create a staff account, go to "Settings > Staff Management" on the device or merchant dashboard. Enter the staff member's name and phone number; they will receive an SMS with a temporary PIN.

Staff accounts have limited permissions by default: they can process transactions and issue receipts but cannot view full transaction history, change settings, or withdraw funds. Only the owner account can grant additional permissions.

Each staff member should use their own PIN when starting a shift. This creates an audit trail showing which staff member processed which transaction, useful for resolving disputes later.

To remove a staff account (e.g. when an employee leaves), the owner must deactivate it from the dashboard immediately to prevent unauthorized access.
