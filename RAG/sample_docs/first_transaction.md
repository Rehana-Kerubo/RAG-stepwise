# Guiding the Merchant Through Their First Transaction

Once the device is activated and KYC is approved, walk the merchant through their first live transaction so they can see the full flow before the agent leaves.

Ask the merchant to enter a small amount (e.g. KES 50) on the device keypad and select "Card" or "Mobile Money" as the payment method, depending on what the customer is using.

Have the merchant ask a staff member or use their own phone to complete the payment as a test customer. Confirm the transaction appears instantly on the device screen with a green checkmark and a receipt option.

Show the merchant how to check their balance and transaction history from the device menu, under "Reports > Today's Transactions."

Explain settlement timing: funds are settled to the merchant's linked bank account or mobile wallet within 24 hours for standard accounts, or instantly for merchants on the premium plan.

If the test transaction fails, check network connectivity first, then confirm the Till number is correctly linked before troubleshooting further.
